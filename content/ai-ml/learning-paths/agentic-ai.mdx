## Outcomes

You will design loops where the model proposes actions, executes tools, and verifies results.

## Topics

- Planning and decomposition.
- Tool schemas, retries, and idempotency.
- Human-in-the-loop and observability.

## Anti-patterns

Unbounded autonomy, missing timeouts, and silent tool failures are the fastest way to ship an unreliable agent.
