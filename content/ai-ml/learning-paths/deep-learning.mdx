## Outcomes

You will know how backpropagation fits together, how to debug training, and how to pick architectures for vision and sequence tasks.

## Core topics

- Tensors, autodiff, optimizers, regularization.
- CNNs and RNN/Transformer basics.
- Practical training: batching, mixed precision, checkpointing.

## Practice

Rebuild a small classifier end-to-end, then swap the backbone and measure the impact on latency and accuracy.
