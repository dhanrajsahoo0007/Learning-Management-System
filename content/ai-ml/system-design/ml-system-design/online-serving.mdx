## Requirements framing

Clarify **latency SLO**, **throughput**, **freshness** of models and features, and **failure modes** (degraded default, cached scores, shadow traffic).

## Architecture sketch

- **Feature store** for low-latency, consistent online features.
- **Model server** with autoscaling and warm pools.
- **Traffic splitting** for canaries and rollbacks.
- **Observability**: latency histograms, error budgets, drift monitors.

## Tradeoffs

Bigger models improve quality but hurt tail latency—optimize the percentile your product actually cares about.
